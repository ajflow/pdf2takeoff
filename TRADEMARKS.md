# Name and marks

"pdf2takeoff", the pdf2takeoff logo and pdf2takeoff.com are trademarks of
A.J Awan, the project maintainer. The Apache-2.0 licence covers the code;
it does not grant any right to use the project's name or marks (Apache-2.0
says so explicitly in §6).

**Fine, no need to ask:** using "pdf2takeoff" to refer truthfully to the
project — "we do our takeoffs in pdf2takeoff", "my firm implements and
supports pdf2takeoff" — in posts, courses, listings or marketing, provided
it's clear you're describing the project rather than claiming to *be* it.
Forks, embeds and derivatives are welcome under the licence; give yours a
name of its own.

**Not fine:** anything implying endorsement or affiliation — "official
pdf2takeoff services", putting the name or logo on a competing product or
service, or registering lookalike domains or handles. If you're unsure
which side of the line you're on, open an issue and ask first.

If you spot a misuse, please open an issue rather than confronting anyone —
the maintainer will handle it.
