What is pdf2takeoff? Takeoff you can check, line by line.

pdf2takeoff is free, open-source construction takeoff software. You open a PDF drawing, set the scale, then measure and count straight on the sheet. Every quantity stays on the drawing as a markup you can check, and the whole takeoff exports to Excel, CSV or back into the PDF for Bluebeam.

  • Reads the scale off the title block or a printed dimension
  • One click fills a room's area; one click counts every matching symbol
  • Compares two revisions and shows what moved
  • Runs from Claude, Cursor or a script through its MCP server and CLI

How to do a takeoff from a PDF.

Four steps, all in the browser. The sample drawing walks you through them in under two minutes.

  1. 01

    Open the drawing

    Drop in a PDF or an image. Multi-page sets keep a scale per sheet.

  2. 02

    Set the scale

    Click Auto-read to take it from the title block, or draw along a known dimension. Press C.

  3. 03

    Measure and count

    Lengths, areas, perimeters, volumes and angles. Fill a room in one click. Scan counts every matching symbol.

  4. 04

    Export

    Excel, CSV or a quantity report, or save the markups into the PDF to open in Bluebeam or Acrobat.

AI and accuracy

AI proposes. You decide.

No AI model can count a floor plan reliably on its own yet, so pdf2takeoff never hands you a bare total. AI counting is optional and runs with your own model key. Every item it finds lands on the drawing as a marker you confirm or delete. It works from the command line and AI agents today, and is coming to the browser.

Questions estimators ask.

Is pdf2takeoff really free?

Yes. There is no account, no paid tier and no file limit. It is open source under Apache-2.0, built and maintained by Flowtivity.

Are my drawings uploaded anywhere?

No. pdf2takeoff runs in your browser and processes the PDF on your computer. It works offline once loaded. The only exception is the optional AI count, which sends a page image to the model provider you choose, and only when you ask it to.

Does it work with Bluebeam Revu?

Yes. Markups save into the PDF as standard Line, Polygon and FreeText annotations with their measurements, so they open in Bluebeam Revu and Adobe Acrobat.

Can AI do my takeoff for me?

Not reliably on its own. The best published model gets 54.3% of floor plan counts exactly right. pdf2takeoff uses AI to propose items you confirm, and flags the ones most likely to be wrong.

Does it work on scanned drawings?

Yes, with care. Scans are read with on-device OCR. Accuracy is lower than on PDFs exported from CAD, and we publish that gap in our benchmarks.

Can I measure a JPG, PNG or screenshot of a plan?

Yes. Open or drop any image (PNG, JPEG, iPhone HEIC photos, TIFF, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF or SVG), or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V or ⌘V. Multi-page TIFF scans open as a set, and a scan that states its resolution keeps its paper size, so the printed scale works. Drop several files at once and they open as one set of sheets. An image only has a paper size when the scan states its resolution, so most images are calibrated from a dimension you know. Markups save into a PDF like any other drawing.

Can I open a DXF or DWG file?

DXF, yes. It opens as a vector drawing at a standard scale (1:50, 1:100 and so on) worked out from the file’s own units, so you can measure straight away without calibrating, Scan counts blocks such as doors and fittings exactly, and text stays searchable. DWG is a closed format: export a DXF or a PDF from your CAD app first.
